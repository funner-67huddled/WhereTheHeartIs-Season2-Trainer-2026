## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Where the Heart is: Season 2**. This trainer assists with relationship progression, choice-driven story paths, gameplay statistics, and save-management features in the narrative-focused sequel.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore relationship routes, branching decisions, alternate outcomes, and multiple endings with less repetition.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Where the Heart is: Season 2 is primarily a choice-driven visual novel rather than an action game. Use the trainer after loading into the game and navigating normally through scenes, menus, and interactive sections.

### Tools & Inventory

The game is not centered on a traditional inventory or equipment system. Trainer options should instead be used for supported relationship, progression, and decision-related values.

### World & Environment

The story continues the Season 1 narrative, following the protagonist as he deals with his father's will, develops relationships with returning characters, meets new women, and faces the consequences of his choices. Save data from Season 1 can be carried into Season 2 through the game's export and import system.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat, weapon, or enemy-battle system. Combat-related trainer functions are not applicable; use the available options for narrative progression, relationships, statistics, and branching decisions.
